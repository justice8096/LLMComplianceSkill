# AI & LLM Software Development Regulations - China

**Last Updated:** 2026-10-11
**Tags:** #ai-regulation #china #generative-ai #algorithm-filing #software-development

---

## Overview

China is the **most actively enforced** AI regulatory environment outside the EU. It was the first country with binding regulations for generative AI (August 2023) and has built a layered system of algorithm registration, content labeling, generative AI service rules, and ethics reviews. Enforcement is routine: fines, app takedowns, and mandatory compliance are real. The amended Cybersecurity Law brought AI into national law effective January 1, 2026. In 2026 the CAC added binding rules for **anthropomorphic (companion) AI services** (in force 15 Jul 2026), ten ministries issued trial **AI ethics review measures**, and nearly 200 AI standards were formalised. A comprehensive AI Law remains a research item on the NPC's 2026 legislative plan with no draft published; earlier expectations of a 2027-2028 statute are unsupported.

## Enacted Regulations (Already in Force)

### Administrative Provisions on Recommendation Algorithms (Mar 2022)
- Regulates algorithm-based content recommendation services
- Mandatory algorithm filing with the Cyberspace Administration of China (CAC)
- Transparency requirements for how algorithms influence content delivery

### Administrative Provisions on Deep Synthesis (Jan 2023)
- Governs deep synthesis technologies (deepfakes, voice cloning, face swapping)
- Mandatory labeling of deep-synthesized content
- Registration and security assessment requirements; roughly 8,000 deep-synthesis algorithms filed by September 2026

### Interim Measures for Generative AI Services (Aug 15, 2023)
- **First binding generative AI regulation globally**
- Applies to providers offering generative AI services to the public in China
- Key requirements:
  - Content must be lawful, truthful, and not harmful to national security
  - AI-generated content must be labeled
  - Mandatory algorithm filing with the CAC
  - LLMs with public opinion attributes must undergo security assessments
  - Mandatory LLM filing (separate from algorithm filing)
  - Providers must implement content moderation and user complaint mechanisms
- **Exemption:** R&D and internal use of generative AI are excluded from compliance requirements
- More than 1,500 generative AI services registered by September 2026 (including DeepSeek, Baidu's ERNIE Bot, Doubao, Yuanbao, Qianwen)

### Measures for Labeling AI-Generated Content (Sept 1, 2025)
- **Explicit labels:** Visible to users; required on text, audio, images, videos, and virtual scenes
- **Implicit labels:** Embedded in metadata; include content attributes, provider name, content number
- Content platforms must verify implicit labels and add explicit labels if AI-generated content is detected
- User agreements must outline labeling obligations
- Users may request content without explicit labels, but implicit labels must remain; providers must retain logs for 6+ months
- CAC reported in February 2026 that platforms had labeled more than 150 billion pieces of AI-generated content

### National Security Standards (Nov 1, 2025)
Three national standards for generative AI security:
1. **Data Annotation Security Specification** - security for training data labeling
2. **Pre-training and Fine-tuning Data Security Specification** - dataset security requirements
3. **Basic Security Requirements for GenAI Services** - operational security for AI services

### Amended Cybersecurity Law (Jan 1, 2026)
- First time AI is referenced in China's national law
- Mandates AI ethics compliance, risk monitoring, and safety assessment
- Supports R&D of algorithms and construction of training data resources/computing infrastructure

### Cross-Border Personal Information Certification (Jan 1, 2026)
- Measures on Certification of Cross-Border Provision of Personal Information in force; CAC recognised three certification bodies in January 2026
- Processors of minors' personal information had to complete PIPL compliance audits and upload results by 31 Jan 2026

### Livestreaming E-Commerce AI Rules (Feb 1, 2026)
- AI-generated human images/videos in livestream commerce must be clearly labeled and continuously disclosed
- Operators using AI-generated characters bear legal liability for violations

### Measures for AI Technology Ethics Review and Service (Trial) (Spring 2026)
- Issued by MIIT with nine other departments; sources give the effective date as 20 Mar 2026 or 3 Apr 2026 (unverified against the MIIT text)
- Pre-development ethics review with application, general, simplified, expert and emergency procedures; six ethical principles
- Extends the December 2023 ethics review regime beyond biomedical research to AI generally

### Interim Measures for Anthropomorphic AI Interactive Services (CAC Order No. 21, in force Jul 15, 2026)
- Adopted 2 Feb 2026, published 10 Apr 2026; co-issued with NDRC, MIIT, MPS and SAMR
- Covers companion, role-play and other human-like conversational AI
- Requirements: AI disclosure at session start; security assessment and filing with the provincial CAC once a service reaches 1 million registered users or 100,000 monthly active users; human takeover on self-harm signals; break prompts after two hours of continuous use; a minor mode with guardian consent; a domestic representative for offshore providers

## Enforcement in Practice

China actively enforces its AI regulations:
- **Qinglang 2026 AI misuse campaign (CAC and MPS, Apr-Sep 2026):** phase-two report of 2 Sep 2026 cited 5.61 million items removed, more than 49,000 accounts penalised and more than 2,400 sites and apps dealt with; Doubao, Yuanbao, Qianwen and ERNIE Bot were named as compliant
- **"Clear and Bright" Campaign (April-June 2025):** over 3,500 AI products taken down, 960,000+ pieces of illegal content scrubbed, 3,700+ accounts penalized
- **Shanghai CAC (June 2025):** AI chatbot apps summoned for generating vulgar content; ordered to improve content review
- **Chongqing CAC (July 2024):** AI services suspended for providing generative AI without completing security assessments and LLM filings

## Upcoming and Proposed Rules

### Comprehensive AI Law (Research only)
- The NPC Standing Committee's 2026 legislative plan (May 2026) lists "healthy development of AI" as a **research** topic, not among the 34 scheduled or 22 backup bills
- The State Council's 2026 plan says it will "accelerate" comprehensive AI legislation, but no draft has been published
- Treat any enactment date as speculative

### Draft Digital Virtual Person Information Service Measures (CAC, Apr 3, 2026)
- Comments closed 6 May 2026; consent for use of a person's likeness; no virtual partners or relatives for under-18s; fines up to RMB 200,000

### Draft Minors' Online Protection and AI Rules (CAC, Sep 18, 2026)
- Would ban virtual intimate-relationship AI for minors, addiction-fostering algorithms and minors hosting livestreams (16+ livelihood exception)

### 15th Five-Year Plan (2026-2030), approved March 2026
- Emphasises "orderly" AI development and the AI Plus diffusion programme (for example the January 2026 AI + Manufacturing implementation opinions)

## Executive Instruments to Watch

| Instrument class | Issuer | Where published | Current mandate ends |
|------------------|--------|-----------------|----------------------|
| Measures, provisions and interim measures (binding) | Cyberspace Administration of China, often co-issued with MIIT, MPS, SAMR, NDRC | cac.gov.cn | State Council and NPC term to Mar 2028 (Xi Jinping; Premier Li Qiang) |
| Ethics review measures, AI Plus implementation opinions, standards assignments | MIIT and co-issuing ministries; TC260 | miit.gov.cn, tc260.org.cn | Mar 2028 |
| Enforcement campaigns (Qinglang) | CAC and MPS | cac.gov.cn | Annual |
| Five-Year Plan and State Council legislative plans | State Council, NPC Standing Committee | gov.cn, npc.gov.cn | 15th Plan 2026-2030 |

In China the executive measure is the binding instrument; a comprehensive AI Law remains at research stage.

Executive instruments (orders, decrees, cabinet decisions, ministry measures, guidelines) do not expire when the issuing leader leaves office; they stay in force until revoked or superseded. The mandate-end date is the point at which revocation risk rises and is the date to re-verify every instrument listed above.

## Standards and Governance Frameworks

- **AI Safety Governance Framework 2.0 (TC260, Sept 2025):** non-binding principles and risk guidance
- **TC260 AI Safety Working Group** created March 2026; 16 AI safety standards assigned in April 2026; a research report proposed eight AI-agent security standards and a second mandatory agent-security standard is moving toward adoption
- **MIIT (Aug 26, 2026):** nearly 200 AI standards formalised, mostly recommended GB/T standards

## AI Ethics Review

### Ethics Review Measures (Dec 2023)
- Mandatory ethical review for AI R&D activities in biological and medical fields
- Expanded in August 2025, then generalised by the 2026 trial AI ethics review measures above

### Patent and IP Rules (Jan 1, 2026)
- Inventors must be natural persons; AI cannot be designated as an inventor
- Patent specifications for AI inventions must describe model architectures, training processes, and key parameters
- AI-generated voices recognized as protected personality rights (Supreme Court, May 2025)

## Key Compliance Requirements for Developers

### If You Offer Generative AI Services in China:
1. **Register algorithms** with the CAC (mandatory)
2. **File LLMs** separately if they have public opinion or social mobilization capabilities
3. **Complete security assessments** before offering public services
4. **Implement content labeling** - both explicit (visible) and implicit (metadata)
5. **Moderate content** - must be lawful and not harmful to national security, social stability
6. **Handle user complaints** - established complaint/reporting mechanisms required
7. **Maintain logs** - retain relevant records for minimum 6 months
8. **Comply with data security standards** - training data, annotation, and pre-training/fine-tuning security
9. **Companion and role-play AI:** disclose at session start, add minor mode, break prompts and self-harm takeover, and file once user thresholds are reached
10. **Run an ethics review** before developing AI covered by the 2026 trial measures

### Penalties
- Administrative fines imposed on companies and responsible individuals
- Business suspension orders
- Criminal liability for the most serious violations
- App/service takedowns and account closures

## What This Means for Developers

1. **China is already enforcing** - this is not a "wait and see" market
2. **Algorithm and LLM filing is mandatory** for public-facing AI services
3. **Content labeling** (explicit + implicit) has been required since September 2025 and is checked in campaigns
4. **Security assessments** must be completed before launching generative AI services
5. **Companion AI is now specifically regulated** - offshore providers need a domestic representative
6. **R&D and internal use** are currently exempt from most compliance requirements
7. **Do not plan around a comprehensive AI law date** - it is still at the research stage
8. **AI-generated content moderation** is taken very seriously - vulgar, false, or politically sensitive outputs trigger enforcement
9. **Patent applications** involving AI must disclose model architecture and training details
10. **Foreign companies** serving Chinese users are subject to these regulations
